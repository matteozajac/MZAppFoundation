# Code of conduct

MZAppFoundation welcomes people with different backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and to interactions where someone represents the project.

## Working together

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and code with specific, constructive feedback.
- Allow room for questions, disagreement, and honest mistakes. Explain your reasoning and accept corrections.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries. Ask before sharing another person's private information or communications.
- Keep participation free of harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual attention, impersonation, and repeated disruptive behavior.
- Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern or helping resolve one.

## Reporting a concern

Contact maintainer Mateusz Zając privately at [matteo.zajac@gmail.com](mailto:matteo.zajac@gmail.com), the contact published on [the maintainer's website](https://matteozajac.com/projects/masseter.html#support). Include the relevant links or a brief description and explain any immediate privacy or safety concern. Share only the information needed to understand the situation; do not post a sensitive conduct report in a public issue.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer will consider the context, impact, and available information, and may ask the people involved for clarification. Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of content, a warning, or a temporary or permanent participation restriction. Serious or repeated harm can justify an immediate restriction.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Information may need to be shared with people necessary to investigate or respond. A person affected by a decision can contact the maintainer with relevant context or new information for reconsideration.
